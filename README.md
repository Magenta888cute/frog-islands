# Frog Islands 🐸🏝️

A 3D frog-collecting game designed by Sasha.

**Play it here: https://magenta888cute.github.io/frog-islands/**

You wake up on a floating island above the clouds with a frog egg in your bag. Put it in the lake: in 30 island
days (one day is 30 seconds, and eggs keep growing while the game is closed) it hatches into a tadpole, grows legs
and hops out as a frog. Collect all 15 kinds of frogs, from the Green Frog to the Fire Frog, the Ice Frog and the
legendary Rainbow Frog. More frogs bring more luck for rare frogs, and new islands appear as your frog family grows,
all the way to the giant Waterfall Island and the Crystal Cave. Do challenges to earn parachute eggs, find the secret egg
on every island, name and feed your frogs, listen to them sing at night, and decorate your islands with flower coins.

Make a wish at the Wishing Well to give your frogs magical looks or hatch one of the 10 Mystic Frogs, dress up the Frog
Keeper, build and decorate a house, pick wildflowers, take photos with your frogs, and carry your save to another device
with *Settings → Move my save*.

Works on iPad, iPhone and computers. On iPad or iPhone, tap Share → *Add to Home Screen* to play it like an app.

This repository holds the built game: `index.html` contains all of the code.
